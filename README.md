\# HR Attrition \& Workforce Risk Dashboard



\## Project Overview



This project analyzes employee attrition patterns using HR workforce data. The dashboard was built in Tableau to help HR leaders identify employee groups with the highest attrition risk and prioritize retention strategies.



The analysis focuses on job role, overtime, age group, and income band to understand where attrition is most concentrated.



\## Business Question



Which employee groups are most at risk of leaving, and where should HR focus retention efforts?

\## Key Insights



\- Overall employee attrition rate is 16.1%.

\- Sales Representatives have the highest attrition rate at 39.8%.

\- Employees working overtime have a much higher attrition rate than those who do not work overtime.

\- Employees under 25 show the highest attrition rate by age group.

\- Low-income employees have significantly higher attrition than mid-income and high-income employees.



\## Recommendations



HR should prioritize retention efforts around:



\- Workload and overtime review

\- Compensation analysis for low-income employees

\- Early-career support and onboarding programs

\- Role-specific retention strategies for high-risk job roles
